# Welcome to SoloMD

A local-first **Markdown** editor — fast, small, and private.

## What you get

- Live preview, split view and reading mode
- Tables, task lists, math and Mermaid diagrams
- An agent panel and MCP server that let your LLM work with your notes

> Plain files on your disk. No lock-in.
